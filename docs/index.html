How to play

  • Swipe in any of the four directions. You slide until a wall stops you — no stopping short, no diagonals.
  • Every tile you cross is swept, and every creature on it is splattered.
  • Sweep 100% of the floor and the exit stairs open.
  • Coins drop where a creature died and wait there. Collecting them is a routing decision, not a reward for the kill.

Frequently asked

Can I get stuck in a level?

No. Every room is carved by a sequence of legal slides, so a valid route always exists — and the game never takes a move back. If a room feels impossible, there is a route you have not spotted yet. The Find Spell (the scroll beside the pause button) draws a trail to the nearest living creature.

What is the difference between the two modes?

  • LEVELS is the campaign: pure sweeping, and nothing in a room can hurt you. Tap the title screen to play.
  • DAILY CHALLENGE is one timed room per day, the same room for every player on earth. Tap the skull on the title screen to play.

Does the game work offline?

Yes, completely. There are no accounts, no timers, no energy and no ads. If you choose to post your scores, Game Center sends them when you next have a connection.

Will the music interrupt my podcast?

Never. Dungeon Splat mixes its audio over whatever you are already listening to and will not pause it. You can also turn SFX and music off independently in Settings.

Where is the shop?

During any level, tap the book at the top right to open the menu, then the shopping-cart tab. Gold packs are on the GOLD shelf.

I bought gold and reinstalled — can I get it back?

No. Gold packs are consumable, so like any consumable purchase they cannot be restored once the app is deleted. If a purchase was charged but the gold never arrived, email us.

My progress disappeared.

Progress is stored on the device only. Deleting the app, or restoring a device from a backup that predates your progress, clears it. We have no server-side copy — if this happens, email us and we will do what we can.
