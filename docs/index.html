How to play

  • Swipe in any of the four directions. You slide until a wall stops you — no stopping short, no diagonals.
  • Every tile you cross is swept, and every creature on it is splattered.
  • Sweep 100% of the floor and the exit stairs open.
  • Coins drop where a creature died and wait there. Collecting them is a routing decision, not a reward for the kill.

Frequently asked

Can I get stuck in a level?

No. Every room is carved by a sequence of legal slides, so a valid route always exists — and the game never takes a move back. If a room feels impossible, there is a route you have not spotted yet. The Tracking Spell will point you at the nearest living creature.

What is the difference between the three modes?

  • LEVELS is the campaign: pure sweeping, and nothing in a LEVELS room can hurt you.
  • ARCADE adds every hazard — spikes, bats, dart gargoyles, the dragon and the sun stone.
  • DAILY DUNGEON is one room per day, the same seed for every player on earth.

Does the game work offline?

Yes, completely. There are no accounts, no timers, no energy and no ads. Game Center scores sync when you next have a connection.

Will the music interrupt my podcast?

Never. Dungeon Splat mixes its audio over whatever you are already listening to and will not pause it. You can also turn SFX and music off independently in Settings.

I bought Infinite Coins and reinstalled — where did it go?

Open the Hero Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Non-consumable purchases are tied to your Apple Account and come straight back. Coin packs are consumable and are spent once used, so they cannot be restored.

My progress disappeared.

Progress is stored on the device only. Deleting the app, or restoring a device from a backup that predates your progress, clears it. We have no server-side copy — if this happens, email us and we will do what we can.
