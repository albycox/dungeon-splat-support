Privacy Policy

Dungeon Splat · Last updated August 20, 2026

Dungeon Splat does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The game is fully playable offline.

What stays on your device

Your progress is saved locally on your device and is never sent anywhere. That includes:

  • Which dungeon floor you have reached
  • Your gold balance and unlocked heroes
  • Your longest splat streak
  • Sound and music preferences

Deleting the app deletes all of it. We have no copy and no way to recover it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Dungeon Splat submits leaderboard scores and achievement progress to Apple's Game Center service. This is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your Apple Account, your real name, or any other personal detail — only the scores Game Center chooses to show. Game Center is optional: declining it does not limit the game in any way.

In-app purchases

Dungeon Splat offers optional in-app purchases for in-game gold and cosmetic items. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details, card number, or billing address — Apple handles the entire transaction and only tells the app whether a purchase succeeded.

Purchases are recorded against your Apple Account by Apple so that non-consumable items can be restored if you reinstall the game. Nothing about your purchases is stored on our servers, because we do not operate any servers.

Everything sold in Dungeon Splat is cosmetic or convenience only. There is no advertising, no energy timer, and no subscription.

Children

Dungeon Splat does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. In-app purchases can be restricted or disabled entirely using Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy controls.

Third parties

Dungeon Splat contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no social media SDKs. The only external service the app talks to is Apple's own Game Center and App Store infrastructure.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
